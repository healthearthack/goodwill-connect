Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview

Goodwill Connect ("we", "our") is dedicated to protecting user privacy across our verifiable volunteer network and HubSpot Technology Partner integrations. This policy outlines how contact records, service hour audits, and credential metadata are handled.

2. Data Synchronization & HubSpot CRM Scopes

When you install the Goodwill Connect app into your HubSpot portal, we only access permissions explicitly authorized via OAuth (Contacts and Companies read/write). We use this data solely to synchronize verified volunteer hours, display CRM UI extension cards, and format CSR impact metrics for your organization.

3. Data Retention & Security

All telemetry and volunteer verification logs are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and stored with strict role-based access control. We never sell or distribute corporate partner data.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy or data management, contact our compliance team at compliance@goodwillindustriesinternational.com.ai.
